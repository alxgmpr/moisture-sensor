### What I want

1. **Sanity-check the footprints** against the parts, especially:
   - U1 nRF54L15-QFAA — vendor footprint `QFN48_6X6_NOR`, 6×6 mm 0.4 mm pitch
   - U2 nPM1300-QEAA — vendor footprint `QFN32_5X5_NOR`
   - U4 SHT45 — must be the `_NoCentralPad` variant, and no copper under it
   - Hand-solder variants are used everywhere **except** the RF matching network
     (L2/L3/L4/C6/C9/C11 are 0201 standard on purpose — see BOM.md)

2. **Draw the board outline.** Form factor is a stake: electronics at the top,
   capacitive probe extending down into the soil. I haven't fixed dimensions —
   propose something that fits the 103450 cell (34 × 50 × 10 mm) and gives the
   probe useful electrode area.

3. **Place components** according to the zoning in LAYOUT.md §4:
   - **Zone A (top): antenna** — copper-free on all four layers, ground plane
     stops at the boundary
   - **Zone B: electronics** — solid In1.Cu ground. RF section at the top next to
     Zone A, switchers (nPM1300 SW2, nRF54L15 DCC) at the bottom, away from RF
   - **Zone C: probe** — **no ground plane at all**, guard pour instead

### Constraints that will bite if missed

- **RF trace is 50 Ω microstrip, W = 0.38 mm** on F.Cu over In1.Cu. No vias in
  the RF path. Matching network as close to U1 pin 31 (ANT) as physically
  possible. Stitching vias ≤ 3 mm along the RF run.
- **C6's ground connects only to pin 32 (VSS_PA) on the top layer**, and pin 32
  connects to pin 49 only underneath the package. **C9's ground connects only on
  the bottom ground layer.** These are the same net in the schematic; the
  isolation is a layout rule. Consider net-ties if you want DRC to enforce them.
- **No ground plane in the probe zone.** Guard pour over ground at 0.2104 mm is
  1.85e-7 F/m², so a 20 cm² probe alone would hit the FDC1004's 400 pF shield
  drive limit.
- **No copper under U4 (SHT45)** except its four pin pads — the die pad acts as a
  heat sink and stops the internal heater meeting spec.
- **Keep sense traces away from ground.** Ground adjacent to a sense net is
  measured capacitance; it eats CAPDAC range. Guard, not ground, surrounds sense.
- Crystals X1/X2 close to their pins, short traces — the INTCAP value excludes
  PCB stray, and C_L is capped at 9 pF with no margin to spare.

### Antenna

PCB inverted-F, geometry copied from **Nordic's QFAA reference layout 0.8**. I
have the package extracted; the PCB and draftsman PDFs are in it. The matching
network values in the schematic are Nordic's and are only valid for their antenna
geometry, so copy the geometry rather than improvising.

Antenna wants ≥ 50 mm above the soil line. Tuning must be done with the enclosure
fitted and a realistic soil load — free-space tuning will be wrong.

### Known gaps

- Crystal ordering codes, L1, and D5 not yet selected — BOM.md has the
  requirements. C_L ≤ 9 pF rules out most 32.768 kHz parts.
- Cell self-discharge rate unverified; it's 92 % of the power budget.
