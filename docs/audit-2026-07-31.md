## 1. `/VSET1` shorts to the ground pour — Error

Two F.Cu segments on `/VSET1` sit at 0.0000 mm from `ZoneB_GND_F`, against a
0.1270 mm rule:

```
@(72.1000, 97.9243)  Track [/VSET1] on F.Cu, length 0.2465 mm
@(72.1000, 100.0025) Track [/VSET1] on F.Cu, length 2.0782 mm
```

`/VSET1` is `R21.1 + U2.17`, and R21's other end is already on GND. A short
from the track to the pour bypasses R21 entirely, so the pin sees 0 Ω to
ground rather than the programmed resistance. On nPM1300 the VSET resistor
selects the buck's default output voltage.

See finding 3 — the cleanest fix may be to delete this net rather than reroute
it.

## 2. Sense electrodes are isolated copper — Error

Four `isolated_copper` warnings:

```
@(69.0, 159.0)  Zone 'SENSE1_electrode'    [/SENSE1] F.Cu
@(69.0, 122.0)  Zone 'SENSE2_electrode'    [/SENSE2] F.Cu
@(67.3, 112.0)  Zone 'ZoneC_GUARD'         [/SHLD]   B.Cu, In2.Cu  (x2)
@(67.0, 105.8)  Zone 'SenseEscape_GUARD'   [/SHLD]   B.Cu, In2.Cu  (x2)
```

The zones carry the correct nets — `SENSE1_electrode`→`/SENSE1`,
`SENSE2_electrode`→`/SENSE2`, both guard zones→`/SHLD`. What is missing is
copper joining each zone to U3's pins: `/SENSE1` (U3.2 CIN1), `/SENSE2`
(U3.3 CIN2) and `/SHLD` (U3.1 SHLD1, U3.6 SHLD2) have no track anywhere on
the board. These three nets are the entire measurement function.

## 3. BUCK1 unused but VSET1 populated — Design question

nPM1300 BUCK1 is not used:

| Pin | Name | State |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | VOUT1 | no-connect |
| 3 | SW1 | no-connect |
| 2 | PVSS1 | GND |
| 17 | VSET1 | R21 → GND |

Only BUCK2 is built out (SW2→L10→VOUT2 = `/+3V3`). With BUCK1 disabled, R21
and the `/VSET1` net configure a regulator that never runs — and that net is
the one shorting to the pour in finding 1. Either drop R21 and `/VSET1`, or
record why the strap is needed. Same question applies to LOADSW2/LDO2
(pins 30, 31 both no-connect) though those have no external parts attached.

For comparison, Nordic's reference layout (`nRF54L15_qfaa_4L_config1_pcb.pdf`)
places C2, C12 and C5 directly against the DECA/DECRF pins, and C3/C10/L1/FB1

three DECA caps here are at 8.2 mm.

**Root cause worth noting:** every support passive is a 0402 HandSolder
footprint where Nordic's reference uses 0201. The larger pads and the
elongated hand-solder geometry are part of why the caps cannot sit as close.
That is an assembly choice, not an error, but it caps how much of this gap is
recoverable.

## 6. BUCK2 switching loop — Layout

Measured from pad geometry, board coordinates:

```
U2.5  SW2    ( 7.074, 56.250)
L10.1        ( 3.275, 53.500)   <- SW2 side
L10.2        ( 4.725, 53.500)   <- VOUT2 side
C24.1 +3V3   ( 3.138, 57.000)
C24.2 PVSS2  ( 4.862, 57.000)
U2.6  PVSS2  ( 7.074, 56.750)

enclosed area  :  5.50 mm²
loop perimeter : 14.43 mm
```

**L10 is oriented backwards.** `/SW2` connects U2.5 to L10.1, which is the pad
*further* from U2 — so the highest-dv/dt net on the board runs past the whole
inductor body. Rotating L10 180° puts the switch pad nearest the IC:

| | SW2 run |
|---|---|
| As placed (U2.5 → L10.1) | 4.69 mm |
| L10 rotated 180° (U2.5 → L10.2) | 3.62 mm |

`/SW2` currently has no copper at all, so this costs nothing to fix now.

The TPS7A1650 was checked for the same thing and does not apply — it is an
LDO, so there is no switching loop. Its cap distances (C30 2.72 mm to IN,
C31 2.62 mm to OUT) are in the table above and are much less critical at
100 mA.

## 7. Crystal routing — Layout

| Net | Pads | Direct span | Routed copper | Overshoot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| XC1 | U1.34 ↔ X2.1 | 5.12 mm | **7.09 mm** | +38% |
| XC2 | U1.35 ↔ X2.3 | 3.76 mm | **5.68 mm** | +51% |
| XL1 | U1.1 ↔ X1.1 | 5.79 mm | 6.75 mm | +17% |
| XL2 | U1.2 ↔ X1.2 | 4.97 mm | 5.52 mm | +11% |

Two problems. The routed lengths are mismatched — **1.41 mm between XC1 and
XC2** on the 32 MHz oscillator and 1.23 mm between XL1 and XL2 — which
unbalances the load presented to each side of the crystal. And both pairs are
long: Nordic's reference places X2 immediately adjacent to XC1/XC2.

Neither crystal has external load capacitors, which matches Nordic's reference
(both use the internal caps) and HARDWARE.md's crystal section. Not a finding.

## Confirmed correct — no action

These were checked and match their references. Recording them so they are not
re-examined.

- **nRF54L15 support topology matches Nordic's reference exactly.** DECA
  (pin 43) and DECRF (pin 33) are tied together with C2/C12/C5 — that is what
  Nordic's own schematic does, and was worth verifying because tying two
  regulator decoupling pins is usually wrong. FB1 bridging DECD→DECA, and
  DCC→L1→DECD with C1, also match.
- **All component values match Nordic's reference**, including the RF matching
  network down to the MPNs (LQP03HQ2N7B02, LQP03HQ3N5B02, GJM0335C1E1R5WB01,
  GJM0335C1E2R0WB01). Packages are upsized 0201→0402 (0603 for C3).
- **C6 and C9 isolated grounds are structurally correct.** Nordic requires C6's
  ground to reach only pin 32 (VSS_PA), and C9's ground to be isolated from all
  ground layers except the bottom. `/GND_PA` (C6.2 + NT1 + U1.32) and
  `/GND_C9` (C9.2 + NT2, NT2 on B.Cu) implement both.
- **RF matching topology** — L2/L3/L4 in series from ANT with C6/C9 shunt and
  C11 shunt at the U.FL feed. Matches the reference.
- **D5 solar ORing polarity** — anode on `/SOLAR_5V`, cathode on `/VBUS_IN`.
  Solar can push into VBUS; USB cannot backfeed the panel.
- **BUCK2 topology** — SW2→L10→VOUT2=`/+3V3`, with C24 returned to
  `/GND_PVSS2` rather than plain GND. Correct.
- **Power gating chain** — `/+3V3` → U2.28 (LSIN1) → U2.29 (LSOUT1) →
  `/FDC_VDD` → U3.8. Matches HARDWARE.md §5.
- **All 57 no-connects are legitimate** — unused nRF54L15 GPIO (34), USB 3.x
  SuperSpeed and sideband pairs on J1 (14), unused nPM1300 BUCK1/LOADSW2/
  VBUSOUT/LED2/GPIO (11 across U2), FDC1004 CIN3/CIN4 (2), TPS7A1650
  DELAY/FB/NC/PG (4), Tag-Connect pins 7–8 (2).
- **ERC: 0 violations.**
- **Annotation: clean.**

## Recommended order for Phase 3

Findings 1, 2, 3 and 6 all sit in the power/PMIC and sense blocks and all
touch nets that currently have no copper. Doing power/PMIC first — as the
design already specifies — lets the L10 rotation, the VSET1 decision and the
BUCK2 loop tightening land before anything is routed around them.

Findings 4, 5 and 7 are the MCU block and are the largest placement change:
pulling C2/C12/C5 in from 8.2 mm, C3/C10 in from 9.4/5.1 mm, C1 in from
5.18 mm, and moving X2 closer with length-matched XC1/XC2.

Finding 2 is the sense block and is the only one that blocks the board's
primary function.
