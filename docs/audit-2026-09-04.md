# Schematic and PCB placement audit — 2026-09-04

The system architecture is reasonable for a duty-cycled BLE plant sensor, but the
nRF support placement is not yet a good match to Nordic's reference layout. The
board passes connectivity and clearance checks; those checks do not establish
adequate decoupling, oscillator margin, RF performance, or waterproofing. Treat
this as a design under development, not a fabrication release.

## Scope and design understanding

Reviewed the current schematic/netlist, live KiCad board, copper/rule areas,
component and pad positions, routing, project constraints, local reference
layout PDFs, sensor datasheets, and existing design notes. The July audit refers
to a retired power architecture and is not the current baseline.

The CR2032 feeds an nPM2100 boost regulator. Its main output powers the nRF54L15,
SHT45, and I2C pull-ups; its switched output gates the FDC1004. The FDC1004 reads
two capacitive probe electrodes with driven shielding. RF exits through U.FL to
an enclosure-mounted antenna. The SHT45 is on a side tab: it measures air
humidity and local ambient temperature, not soil water content or buried soil
temperature. Soil moisture is inferred from capacitance and requires calibration.

The broad floorplan is sensible: RF at one end, battery centrally located, PMIC
below the battery, capacitance converter at the probe shoulder, and humidity
sensor outside the main electronics area. The highest-priority improvements
are within the nRF support cluster, rather than moving the main functional blocks.

saved by KiCad. The schematic capacitor addition was initially a file edit;
KiCad's GUI subsequently loaded it and **Update PCB from Schematic** reconciled

Existing uncommitted work was retained. Before-edit design files are saved in

## Placement findings, in priority order

### High: rebuild the nRF decoupling/filter cluster around the pins

The schematic topology has DCC → L1 → DECD → FB1 → DECA, with DECRF tied to
DECA. The placement does not implement this as the compact local network shown
in the supplied Nordic QFAA reference layout.

| Supply connection | Straight-line pad-centre separation |
|---|---:|
| C1.1 to U1.45, DECD | 5.185 mm |
| C2.1 to U1.43, DECA | 5.081 mm |
| C5.1 to U1.33, DECRF | 8.248 mm |
| C10.1 to U1.47, VDD | 5.324 mm |
| C4.1 to U1.36, VDD | 1.933 mm |
| C7.1 to U1.22, VDD | 1.320 mm |
| C8.1 to U1.10, VDD | 1.179 mm |

These are geometric distances, not extracted electrical loop inductance. C7 and
C8 are substantially better placed than C1/C2/C10. The DECA net contains
25.24 mm of total trace across its branches and three vias, including a long
bottom-layer detour between the IC and the capacitor cluster. This number is
not a single pin-to-capacitor path length. DECD has 6.61 mm of total trace.

Re-place C1/C2/C5/C12/FB1 and C10 as one group, using the approved Nordic-sized
0201 parts and standard L1 lands. Place C1 at DECD; C2/C12 at the DECA filter;
C5 at DECRF; C10 at the adjacent VDD47/48 pins. Give the capacitor grounds short
connections and adjacent vias into the local ground reference. Preserve each
specified value rather than combining the capacitors. Move the I2C pull-ups out
of this valuable local placement area if necessary. Then route the sensitive
supply connections before the digital bus.

This coordinated change remains open. Merely shrinking the present footprints
in place would leave the long electrical paths. The exact silicon order code
and matching Nordic reference revision also remain to be locked in the project.
The supplied reference is `doc/nRF54L15_qfaa_4L_config1_pcb.pdf`.

### High: rework X2 with the adjacent supply and RF placement

XC1 is 3.95 mm on F.Cu with no vias. XC2 is 6.00 mm and uses two through vias
and a B.Cu segment. The asymmetric layer usage and additional parasitic loading
are avoidable. This is an oscillator network, so matching length with serpentine
routing is not the objective; short, quiet, similar connections are.

Reorient/reposition X2 and the adjacent C4/C5 network so both oscillator nets
remain on F.Cu and each grounded crystal pad has a short return. Do this with
the nRF support re-placement above; retain the correctly assigned crystal pad
numbers. Verify startup and trim in firmware with the selected crystal. X1's
nearby two-pad placement is less problematic and was left intact.

### Medium: PMIC floorplan is serviceable; retain and validate its local loop

The nPM2100, L10, and capacitors occupy a compact region below the cell. The
small input and output capacitors are closer than their bulk partners. C22's
supply pad is 1.139 mm from VBAT; C24's supply pad is 1.765 mm from VINT15.
The nRF DCC switching connection itself is short, about 1.58 mm of trace.

The PMIC region already has an exposed-pad ground via field and a nearby In1
reference plane. The next improvement would be to bring C24/C23 and their
returns as close as practical to VINT/PVSS while preserving the battery removal
courtyard. There was no clear reason to disturb this entire power block in this
pass. Nordic identifies the boost output capacitor loop as especially important
and discourages splitting its power/analog grounds. See the supplied
`doc/datasheets/nPM2100_HW_Design_Guidelines_nwp_058.pdf`, §4.2.

Exact-part DC-bias, tolerance, temperature and aging checks remain required for
C21/C23/C25. Nominal microfarads do not prove effective capacitance. Confirm
startup and BLE bursts with an aged/high-impedance CR2032 before making a
battery-life claim. The documented absence of reverse-cell electronics is an
existing product decision, not something addressed by this placement pass.

### Medium: RF geometry still needs reference-layout review and measurement

The RF series path stays on F.Cu without signal vias. C6 retains its local
VSS_PA return and under-package ground tie; C9 retains a separate bottom-layer
return. C11 now has a much shorter ground connection. Preserve those distinct
return arrangements during further work. Nordic specifically calls out C6's
return through pin 32 as part of harmonic filtering. [Nordic PCB layout example](https://docs.nordicsemi.com/r/bundle/ps_nrf54l15/page/chapters/ref_circuitry.html-layout).

Do not treat the 0.1565 mm trace rule as proof that every matching-network
segment is a 50-ohm transmission line: the deliberately removed inner copper
under the filter differs from the controlled-reference feed to U.FL. Copy the
matching reference geometry closely, then validate the populated path. The
nominal stack specifies 0.0994 mm top dielectric; final pressed stack and
impedance geometry need fabrication confirmation.

The enclosure antenna, cell, coax and wet soil form part of the RF environment.
The stored antenna-wall spacing is a design intent, not a mechanical/EM test
completed by this audit. VNA and radiated performance checks remain open.

### Medium: sensing and environmental interfaces need prototype validation

U3 is sensibly placed at the probe shoulder. SENSE1 and SENSE2 remain on F.Cu
without signal vias, with driven-shield copper. Their trace totals are about
49.94 mm and 12.81 mm respectively because the electrodes occupy different
parts of the blade; they are not a differential pair requiring equal lengths.
In1 ground extends to the shoulder region, with local rule areas around the
sensing pins. Preserve reference ground around the digital side while keeping
unwanted grounded copper away from the sensing paths.

SHLD1 and SHLD2 share the SHLD net. This must remain consistent with the
single-ended measurement configuration; do not independently configure opposing
shield waveforms on these tied pins. Inspect shield capacitance and input range
on the coated probe in dry and wet soil. The broad shielding planes make load
and coating effects worth measuring rather than estimating from DRC.

The SHT45 tab and its body copper keepout are helpful. The new capacitor adds
no plane beneath the sensor body. Thermal response and humidity access still
depend on the enclosure opening and sealing method. Keep the sensing membrane
free of coating and contaminants. Sensirion's guidance emphasizes thermal
isolation and suitable exposure. [Sensirion design-in guide](https://sensirion.com/resource/user_guide/sht/design_in/).

A production insertion mark/stop, sealed FR-4 edges and probe entry, coating
process, and final-assembly calibration remain open mechanical/process items.
An enclosure rating alone does not characterize the completed slotted assembly.

## Schematic and documentation findings

The revised power tree and the checked net assignments agree between PCB and
schematic. The nPM2100 startup/firmware-controlled rail sequencing, switched FDC
supply, reset connection, I2C pull-ups, and radio support circuitry should be
validated together during bring-up. ERC alone does not test firmware sequencing
or powered-off leakage. No firmware changes or bench tests were made here.

The only intentional netlist change in this pass is C28.1 added to
`+3V3_FDC_SW` and C28.2 added to `GND`. The netlist fingerprint was checked for
those exact additions and refreshed. C26 retains X7R; the BOM's older proposed
X5R substitute is not implicitly approved by this audit.

The older implementation log said connections remained unrouted. The actual
starting board was already fully routed, including the XC2 bottom-layer detour.
Use the dated reports below rather than that stale status.
