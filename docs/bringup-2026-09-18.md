## Failure investigation

An earlier RTT diagnostic capture reported `sensor_start rc=-116` during the
first PMIC/FDC startup attempt. The PMIC voltage readings were valid, and the
same path succeeded after retrying. A 250 ms post-battery settle delay plus up
to three retries for `-ETIMEDOUT` were added. Later cold-power diagnostics
completed initialization, measurement, and radio startup successfully.

The more persistent symptom was that a direct BLE scan sometimes saw no packet
after a battery power cycle. The firmware was using a slow advertising
interval while advertising for only two seconds. That window could expire
before the first advertisement event. Development advertising now uses the
fast interval.

## Direct BLE evidence

The device was observed as `Soil-402A` with service UUID FCD2. An 80-second
direct scan after flashing the fast-interval image found it 11 times. Example
calibrated BTHome service data:

```text
4002830803f8100c220b140000140000
```

A final 15-second scan found it four times:

```text
40027c080317110c220b140000140000
```

The packets contain temperature, humidity, voltage, and two calibrated
moisture objects. The old raw-capacitance packet format was not present in the
final image.

## Home Assistant evidence

Home Assistant was reachable at `10.1.3.4`. Its existing BTHome device is
still displayed as `ProbeQA 402A`, using BLE address `CA:D7:1C:65:40:2A`.
That is the cached Home Assistant name; the current packet format is
calibrated and the direct scanner identifies the device as `Soil-402A`.

Recorder history showed successful updates at mixed intervals: approximately
61 seconds, 124 seconds, 249 seconds, and occasional multi-minute gaps. This
is consistent with a 2-second battery-device advertising window being missed
by Home Assistant's distributed Bluetooth scanners. The direct Mac scanner,
which actively scans, observed repeated packets during the same firmware test.

The development advertising window is therefore increased from 2 seconds to
10 seconds. This increases the advertising portion of development energy by
about 5x in the simple window-length model, but does not change the 60-second
measurement cadence or production behavior. The radio sleeps between BLE
advertising events, so total battery impact is lower than a continuous-radio
5x assumption.
